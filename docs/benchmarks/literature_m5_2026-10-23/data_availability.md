## Khoshnejad: extraction-ready first case

The [eight-site addendum](khoshnejad2018_evidence_addendum.md) records direct text percentages, source-specific assembly evidence, exact reference residue joins and two unchanged scan environments. Its [JSON](khoshnejad2018_observations.json) retains assay conditions, source location per value, missing N/error reasons, printed literature structural descriptors, exact CSV field strings/lines and input/output hashes.

No site-level expression/activity endpoint is inferred. S6 denaturing gels cover only sites 2/5/9; S7 +/-pAzF expression fractions cover site 5. S3–S5 cover all eight but do not quantify an assembled fraction. Site-5 downstream antibody conjugation, DLS/TEM, targeting, biodistribution and imaging are available in K-M Figures 2–5 and K-S Tables S1/S2 (p6), with experiment-specific conditions. Those tables contain printed downstream numbers (some table cells are image-based); they are available for a later targeting-specific extraction, not missing eight-site TLC replicates.

## Wilding: source access and condition separation

The [original-source addendum](wilding2018_context_addendum.md) and [JSON](wilding2018_observations.json) establish the original source of three stability observations and distinguish every first-batch no-PEG, 5 kDa, and 20 kDa endpoint. Keep PEGylation efficiency, activity, absolute control Tm, ΔTm relative to matched WT and ΔTm,conj as different variables. PEG-conjugate activity is mixture-derived using the paper's equations, not directly measured activity of a purified conjugate.

The SI contains experimental supporting figures S1–S4 and simulation detail/S5, not a per-site thermal-shift numeric table. S1 is a representative gel/autoradiogram, not all raw densitometry. The main text's Associated Content refers to S1–S9, whereas the supplied ten-page SI labels S1–S5; videos are listed separately and were not supplied. Do not claim the complete publisher attachment set has been audited.

The numeric control Tm values are explicitly reported and extracted as context. Mutant bar means/SD remain null pending digitization. Wilding SD versus Wilkerson SEM is preserved as an unresolved provenance distinction. No earlier visual estimates enter the records.

## Schinn: a separate chemical scope, with source discrepancies

S-M p2 (2413) identifies **pPa**, an alkyne-bearing p-propargyloxyphenylalanine. S-S section 5.3, body paragraphs 204–205, identifies pEVOL-pPrF, 2 mM pPa, E. coli-derived CFPS and overnight 30 °C incubation. This chemical identity must not be relabeled pAzF or scored using its library. S-M p5 (2416), Methods names a prior T4L construct as 7LZM and a C-terminal GFP11 fusion; that reference has not been prepared/mapped in this round.

The declared list is N2, D10, G12, K16, T21, I27, H31, K35, S38, A42, S44, K48, N55, D61, I78, A82, T109, T115, R119, R125, A129, Q141, A146, T157 (S-M p2). S-S section 3, Table 1 has 23 primer rows: it omits T21/K35/A129 from that declared set and adds R95/A112. S-S section 5.1 paragraph 197 prints “R1125”, while the main list and primer row say R125. These are recorded discrepancies, not silently corrected site assignments. The expression/activity figure, primers and full construct should be reconciled before an exact sitewise benchmark table is finalized. Main text prose and Figure 1 labels also highlight different top-performing sets; no ranking is inferred from those descriptions.

S-M p2 explicitly states I27 and R125 did not express sufficiently to be screened. Retain them as attempted, low-expression outcomes with unavailable activity; absence from the 22-site activity analysis is informative assay censoring, not untested or zero activity. G12/I78/A146 have reported reduced activity, not automatically “failed” incorporation. Specific activity is activity-assay fluorescence divided by yield-assay fluorescence, then normalized to T4L-GFP11 (S-M Figure 1C; S-S sections 5.3.1–5.3.2, paragraphs 208/211). Split-GFP reports full-length product in this assay context, not direct chemical incorporation fidelity.

Figure 1 caption states triplicates and SD; it does not establish biological independence. SI section 4 validates assay response and triplicates, but is not raw per-mutant data. SI 2.2 compares linear-template and plasmid screens: preserve those methods as different experimental conditions, and do not count their agreement as independent protein/site examples. Main Figure 3B explicitly reanalyzes Albayrak and Swartz 2013 sfGFP data (10 sites); those are reused external observations, not new Schinn T4L measurements. No pPa-to-pAzF pooling is permitted.

## López: tethering, spin-label context, and chemistry controls

L-M p2 (7068) specifies C54T/C97A pseudo-WT background. Added cysteine-derived R1/R8/RX spin labels, cavity mutations 121A/133A, pAcF examples and attachment chemistry must remain part of each construct identity. L-S p9 Figure S2A distinguishes pAzF sites 44/68/72/109/131 and their specific spin-label combinations. R1 positions 128–135 in a helix scan are **spin-label reporters**, not eight independent pAzF sites. Position 65 in L-S S1 is an azide-reduction control experiment, not the same tethering-site screen.

L-S p3 describes pAzF biotinylation with 10–20 equivalents DBCO-biotin and 50–100 µM spin-labeled protein in PBS pH 7.2, 2–4 h at room temperature or overnight at 4 °C. L-S S2B labels a specific example at 100 µM protein, 2 mM reagent, 1 h at 25 °C; retain that figure-specific condition instead of silently substituting the generic protocol. L-S pp3–5 distinguishes streptavidin capture and direct DBCO-bead coupling, with binding efficiency determined from supernatant/wash A280. L-M Figure 2 distinguishes two covalent linker lengths and biotin-mediated schemes. These are not interchangeable with small-molecule conjugation in hFTL or PEGylation in Wilding.

L-M pp2–3 explicitly reports aggregate ranges/approximate levels (80–100% direct tethering, approximately 10% WT* nonspecific binding, approximately 90% or greater biotinylated-protein capture, no more than 5% nonbiotinylated control). These are not exact per-construct measurements and are not substituted for S2 bar heights. L-S p6/S1 reports pAzF reduction by DTT/TCEP and loss of DBCO-biotin reaction under reduction; this is a tested chemical-treatment effect, not failure of an unmodified pAzF site. Exact replicate counts and uncertainty definitions remain unreported for these candidate endpoints.

The first benchmark scope should be qualitative/condition-specific tethering and structural preservation with numeric S2 efficiencies pending digitization and construct reconstruction. EPR line shapes, DEER distributions and halothane titrations require their own endpoints and analysis; they are not stability ΔTm. L-M p4 (7070), Figure 3 explicitly reprints prior spectra for buffer/sucrose/random CNBr attachment from reference 7, Fleissner et al. 2011: mark those control traces as reused. No cross-paper match to Wilding T4L stability measurements is established merely from shared residue numbers.

## Reddington: selection-biased sfGFP and photophysics

R-S p7 Table 1 lists 25 union sites: P13, V16, L18, G20, A37, L44, P56, W57, L60, P75, P89, E95, L119, R122, S147, V150, A154, L178, P187, P192, P196, L201, Q204, P211, F223. The **azF checkmarks** are P13, V16, L18, G20, A37, L44, W57, L60, P75, P89, E95, L119, A154, L178, P187, P192, P196, L201, P211, F223, verified against the rendered table. Blank ncAA cells are absence of a selected hit, not tested negatives. P56/R122/S147/Q204 lack azF selection ticks but Table 2 reports azF spectral entries from later characterization; selection and retesting must remain separate records. V150 is initially an ioF hit, G20 an azF hit; R-S p4 says both failed to produce fluorescent sfGFP in liquid cultures in the presence of either tested aromatic ncAA. Preserve that statement and condition-specific retest evidence; do not invent a full library-negative set or infer lack of protein/incorporation from fluorescence.

R-S p9 Table 2 supplies **printed numerical** excitation/emission shifts relative to parent sfGFP, not relative intensities. ND means not determined and must not become zero; a printed zero is a measured/reported zero shift. R-S p14 Table 3 supplies explicit L44azF extinction coefficients before irradiation and percentage changes under specified redox agents; these are quantitative photophysical endpoints that can be transcribed in a subsequent site-specific table without plot digitization. Their exact N/error is not supplied. Main/SI intensity-time curves still require formal digitization if used quantitatively.

R-M p2 (1160) distinguishes random-library selected hits from deliberately targeted T203, which was not recovered in the original screen. R-M Figure 6 names parent sfGFP structure 2B3P; no new GFP preparation, chromophore handling, or scan is performed here. Preserve nonstandard codon-boundary changes printed for L18/G20/A37/L201/Q204 and the A154/CGC annotation discrepancy in Table 1 until construct sequences are checked. Do not assume every selected clone is a clean isolated single substitution.

R-S pp3–4 describes TOP10/pBAD-sfGFP/pDULE expression with 1 mM ncAA, followed by fluorescence measurements on cells, lysate, or purified protein as specified. Photolysis protocols differ in nominal lamp description: generic SI p4 describes a 6 W UVM-57 lamp spanning 275–380 nm, peak 310 nm; individual figure captions describe 302 nm irradiation. Record the relevant caption and generic method separately. Dark state, UV dose/time, buffer/pH, redox agent and sample format strongly affect the fluorescence endpoints; a nonphotoswitching but fluorescent variant is not a failed incorporation example. R-S pp5–6 discusses ambiguous photoproduct masses and incomplete peptide detection in mass spectrometry: chemical identity claims need those caveats.

R-M p6 (1164) compares T203 behavior with previously reported Y66azF. The prior Reddington et al. 2013 photochemistry study is listed as main reference 9 and SI reference 6; earlier 2012 work is also cited. Prior-study measurements are external context unless explicitly traced to a new measurement; do not count the comparison as an additional 2015 observation. Figures and tables within this paper often describe the same variant/condition; do not count a spectrum, its tabulated peak shift, and a plot of that spectrum as independent observations.
