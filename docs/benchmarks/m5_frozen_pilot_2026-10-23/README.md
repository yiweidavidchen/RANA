Six nonduplicate, reference-mapped T4L stability observations are included conditionally in the descriptive pilot (five Wilding, one new Wilkerson). None is claimed to have a fully sequence-matched experimental/reference construct. Nine figure presentations resolve to those six observations. Eight ferritin conjugation observations remain a separate endpoint/protein cohort.

Here, independent counts mean nonduplicate underlying site/condition observations. They do not establish statistical independence across sites, shared controls, constructs or biological replicates.

CSV blanks mean missing or not applicable, not zero. Numeric graph estimates are rounded to one decimal degree Celsius; SD and SEM are retained as different quantities. Image-reading bounds are separate from experimental error. No new RANA run, score, threshold, rotamer library, overall rank, significance test, pooled correlation or binary performance statistic is introduced.
