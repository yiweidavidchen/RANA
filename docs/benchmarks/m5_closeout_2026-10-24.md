## What the evidence supports and does not support

The five-site Wilding primary cohort and six-site cross-study sensitivity cohort
describe rank associations within selected T4L observations; they are not an
independent test set or a preregistered evaluation. Shared protein, control and
study conditions limit independence. Leave-one-out sensitivity exposes leverage;
it does not justify removing a site. Low-clash fraction is a count fraction of
12 sampled conformers at the frozen geometric cutoff, not experimental success
probability. Coefficient magnitude alone cannot establish superiority over RSA.

Ferritin is a separate eight-site TLC fluorophore-conjugation case, retaining
the original endpoint denominator. Assembly remains primary, monomer sensitivity
only, with I5 and L155 disagreements retained. It does not measure incorporation
efficiency or labeled fraction per subunit. Human checking of digitized bars
does not experimentally validate RANA's algorithms, ranks or tiers.

Unconfirmed full constructs/tags/junctions, the reference-versus-experiment
background differences, missing exact N, reused Wilding/Wilkerson SD/SEM ambiguity,
ferritin author-used PDB uncertainty and undefined TLC errors remain documented
limitations. They constrain stronger scientific claims, not completion of this
explicitly limited retrospective pilot. No wet experiments are required to close
M5 or to execute the proposed M6 scope.

## Remaining gaps assigned to M6

The [M6 plan](m6_plan.md) covers the insertion-code fix and regressions,
same-protein reference-structure sensitivity, continued ferritin assembly/monomer
assessment, a BglB pAzF computational selection case with functional annotation,
and portable input preparation/scanning. None is implemented in this round.
These deferred items must not be described as already completed M5 capabilities.

Current reproducibility is **curated tables to analysis, replayed in the recorded
installed environment**. A clean installation test and a portable complete
paper/structure-to-scan pipeline have not been established. Missing historical
T4L execution provenance remains missing; any later rerun is a new record.

frozen plan, source/calibration records, CSV values and original analysis outputs
remain historical snapshots. Their earlier pending statements do not negate the
